Questions people ask

What equipment do we need?

For a lobby, any TV or display with an HDMI input and a USB webcam. We bring the laptop. For remote staff, their own laptop with a built-in camera is enough.

Does anything get installed on employee laptops?

No. The remote version opens in a browser. Nothing is downloaded and no camera footage is stored or sent anywhere. Tracking happens on the device.

Can we change the deities, the dolls or the backdrop?

Yes. The shrine, the deities, the flowers and the backdrop can all be changed, and your logo and welcome message can be added.

How much notice do you need?

One week for a lobby day with the standard shrine. Two to three weeks for a branded version. Deepavali bookings should be confirmed by the last week of October.

Do you travel outside Chennai?

Yes, with travel costs added to the quote. The remote version works anywhere.
