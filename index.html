What we do

Freelance Unity & XR development

End-to-end development, prototyping and feature work for 2D/3D games and immersive VR and AR applications.

Troubleshooting & problem solving

On-demand support to fix broken C# code, physics and logic bugs, build errors and frame-rate issues.

How we work

  1. 01

    Scope & assess

    We review the project, reproduce the issue or map the feature set, and agree on a clear deliverable and timeline.

  2. 02

    Build & iterate

    Focused development sprints in Unity with regular builds, so you see working results rather than status reports.

  3. 03

    Hand over & train

    Clean, documented code plus a walkthrough session so your team can maintain and extend the work.

Typical engagement: one-off fix · feature sprint · ongoing support retainer
