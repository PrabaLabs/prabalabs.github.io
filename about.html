About

An engineering-led studio that builds what people can touch without touching.

PrabaLabs is an interactive tech studio in Chennai. We build real-time 3D and XR products in Unity, and we use the same tools to make festivals and workplace events something people take part in rather than watch.

Founder

Prabakaran R

Founder, PrabaLabs · Unity XR developer, Chennai

Prabakaran is a Unity XR developer who specialises in gesture-based interaction built on computer vision. He has shipped more than seven gesture-controlled games, four of them on the Microsoft Store, and has built VR environments for neurodiverse, elderly and special-needs users at IIT Madras and SSN Institutions.

He currently works as a Senior VR Developer and Project Associate at IITM Pravartak Technologies Foundation, the technology innovation hub at IIT Madras. PrabaLabs is where that research-grade work on controller-free, real-time interaction becomes products for workplaces, events and studios.

How we think

Reproduce first

Before fixing or building anything, we make the problem happen on our own machine. Guesswork is expensive.

Measure, then build

Frame rate, tracking confidence, time-to-first-interaction. If it matters, it gets a number.

Working builds, not status reports

Every sprint ends with something you can run. That is true for a VR app and for a lamp in a lobby.
