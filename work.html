Case study

Virtual Golu: light the lamp with your hands

Navaratri Golu is a ritual people take part in, not one they watch. With teams spread across cities, half an office only ever sees a photo of the lobby Golu. We built a shrine that reads hand gestures from a webcam so every person can light the kuthuvilakku and offer flowers themselves.

Built for
Office lobbies, town halls and remote teams
Interaction
Hold-to-light gesture with progress ring, fist-to-open-palm flower throw, choice of rose, marigold or jasmine
Stack
Unity, webcam hand tracking, real-time particles and lighting
Status
Pilots running Navaratri 2026. Deepavali version in progress.
